Employment & HR

Employment Basics Foreign Employers Ask About Most

Contracts, probation, social insurance and the operational habits that keep HR clean.

Executive Summary

  • Written contracts and a defined probation period are the foundation of a clean start.
  • Social, health and unemployment insurance are mandatory and schedule-driven.
  • Good documentation habits prevent most disputes before they start.

The Mandatory Safety Net

Employer-side social, health and unemployment insurance are not optional extras; they are statutory, with deadlines and caps that change the real cost of every hire.

Paperwork That Pays for Itself

Clear contracts, signed onboarding documents and consistent records make exits, audits and disputes dramatically cheaper to handle.
