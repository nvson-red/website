Your First 20 Employees in Vietnam: What Should the Organization Look Like?

The sequence of roles that keeps an early Vietnam team lean without creating gaps in capability.

Executive Summary

  • Start with the roles that unblock everyone else: a team lead and an operations anchor.
  • Hire senior before junior so early people define standards instead of inheriting them.
  • Delay specialist roles until the work actually needs them.

Sequence, Not Headcount

The first twenty hires are an architecture problem, not a headcount problem. The order you hire in determines whether the team can operate day to day without a foreign HQ managing every detail.

The First Roles That Matter

A local lead who can make day-to-day calls, and a people-ops anchor who keeps payroll, compliance and onboarding running, buy everyone else the room to focus on the product.
